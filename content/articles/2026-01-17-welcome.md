# Welcome to Mississippi News Group

This is a **sample article** to demonstrate the CMS functionality. You can:

- Edit this article from the admin panel at `/admin`
- Create new articles with images, excerpts, and full Markdown content
- Schedule articles for future publication
- Mix your own articles with the RSS feed from your network sites

## Getting Started

1. Go to `/admin` on your site
2. Log in with your Netlify Identity credentials
3. Click "Articles" in the sidebar
4. Create, edit, or delete articles as needed

Your articles will appear in the main feed alongside stories from your other sites, sorted by date with newest first.
