About Mississippi News Group

Mississippi News Group is a network of 17 local news and sports websites covering communities across Mississippi. Since 2013, our newsrooms have reported the stories that matter close to home — community news, schools, local government, events and sports.

This page brings every newsroom's latest headlines together in one place, so you can follow Mississippi news statewide or go straight to your own community: DeSoto County, Oxford, Tupelo and Lee County, Corinth and Alcorn County, Ripley and Tippah County, New Albany and Union County, Booneville and Prentiss County, Pontotoc, Benton County and the Mississippi Delta.
